【Unit 2】Simple Past / Past Progressive
─────────────────────────────────
◆ Simple Past
  用法：过去完成，与现在无关
  标志：yesterday / ago / last... / in 2010
  例：I saw him yesterday.
  陷阱：有具体过去时间 → 不用 have done

◆ Past Progressive
  用法：过去某时刻正在做
  标志：at 8pm / while / when + 短动作
  例：I was sleeping when phone rang.
  陷阱：when + 短动作(did) , while + 长动作(was doing)
       状态动词 (know/like) 不用进行时

【Unit 3】Simple Past / Present Perfect / Present Perfect Progr.
─────────────────────────────────
◆ Present Perfect
  用法：①经历 ②结果 ③持续到现在
  标志：ever/never/yet/already/just/for/since
  例：I have been to Japan. (经历)
      She has lost her keys. (结果)
  陷阱：for + 时间 → 动作还在(完成时) / 已结束(过去时)
       been to (去过回来) vs gone to (去了未回)

◆ Present Perfect Progressive
  用法：过去持续到现在，强调过程
  标志：for/since/lately/recently
  例：I have been reading for 3 hours.
  陷阱：强调“一直做”不强调结果；
       若强调完成结果 → 用现在完成时

◆ 时间框架陷阱（this morning/today/this week）
  若时间未结束（还在上午）→ have done
  若时间已结束（已是下午）→ 过去时
  例：现在10点 → I have had 3 cups.
      现在下午 → I had 3 cups.

【Unit 4】Past Perfect / Past Perfect Progressive
─────────────────────────────────
◆ Past Perfect
  用法：过去的过去（先发生的）
  标志：by the time / before / after / when + 过去时
  例：By the time I arrived, train had left.
  陷阱：by the time / when 后面只用一般过去时（设坐标）
       主句才用 had done

◆ Past Perfect Progressive
  用法：过去某点之前一直在做
  标志：by the time / for + 过去时间
  例：He was tired because he had been working all night.
  陷阱：强调持续过程和后果，常与 for 连用

【Unit 5】Future / Future Progressive
─────────────────────────────────
◆ Future（will / be going to）
  用法：预测 / 临时决定 / 计划
  标志：tomorrow / next... / in the future
  例：Look at clouds → It is going to rain.
      Phone rings → I will answer.
  陷阱：已有计划（机票已买）→ 现在进行时 (I am flying)
       临时/预测 → will / going to

◆ Future Progressive
  用法：将来精确时刻正在做
  标志：at 8pm tomorrow / this time next week
  例：At 9pm I will be watching a movie.
  陷阱：有精确时间点（at...）→ 必须用 will be doing

◆ 时间状语从句（when / as soon as / until / if）
  结构：主句 will + 从句 现在时
  例：I will call you when I arrive.
  陷阱：从句里绝对不用 will（不说 when I will arrive）

【Unit 7】Tag Questions（反意疑问句）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 基础：前肯后否，前否后肯
  例：You are a student, aren't you?
      You aren't, are you?
  陷阱：助动词抄袭前面（be/do/have/can）

◆ 祈使句（Close the door）→ will you?
◆ Let's... → shall we?
◆ I am... → aren't I? （绝不写 am not I）
◆ 否定词（never/hardly/nobody）→ 后面用肯定附加
  例：He never eats meat, does he?
◆ There is... → isn't there? （用 there，不用 it）

【Unit 8】Additions（So / Too / Neither / Not either / But）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 肯定回应（我也是）：So + 助 + 主 / 主 + 助 + too
  例：I love coffee. So do I. / I do too.
◆ 否定回应（我也不是）：Neither + 助 + 主 / 主 + 助 + not either
  例：I don't like tea. Neither do I. / I don't either.
◆ 表示不同（But）：
  前肯后否：Ana likes fish, but Eva doesn't.
  前否后肯：Ana doesn't like meat, but Eva does.
  陷阱：助动词必须与前面时态一致

【Unit 17】Passive（被动语态）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 结构：be + 过去分词 (done)
  例：The letter was written by Mary.
      The house is being painted.
  陷阱：不及物动词（happen/arrive/die）无被动
       若没必要提动作发出者 → 省略 by...

═══════════════════════════════════

【Unit 18】Passive with Modals（情态被动）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 结构：Modal + be + done
  例：Homework must be finished today.
      This can be done easily.
  陷阱：绝对不漏 be（不能说 must done）

═══════════════════════════════════

【Unit 19】Passive Causative（使役被动）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 结构：have / get + 宾语 + done（让别人做）
  例：I had my hair cut yesterday.
      We got the house painted.
  陷阱：词序固定：have + 事/物 + done
       不是 have done it，而是 have it done

═══════════════════════════════════

【Unit 20 & 21】Real Conditional（真实条件句）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 现在真实（真理/习惯）：
  结构：If + 现在时，现在时
  例：If you heat ice, it melts.
◆ 将来真实（可能发生）：
  结构：If + 现在时，will + 原形
  例：If it rains tomorrow, I will stay home.
  陷阱：if 从句用现在时，绝不用 will
       unless = if not（Unless you study, you won't pass.）

═══════════════════════════════════

【Unit 22】Unreal Conditional（非真实 / 虚拟）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 现在/将来虚拟（与事实相反）：
  结构：If + 过去时，would + 原形
  例：If I were you, I would accept.
      If I had money, I would travel.
  陷阱：所有人称的 be → were（绝对不说 If I was）
◆ Wish：
  现在愿望：wish + 过去时（I wish I were rich）
  过去后悔：wish + 过去完成时（I wish I had studied）

【Unit 24 & 25】Indirect Speech（间接引语）
─────────────────────────────────
◆ 时态回退（倒推一格）：
  现在时 → 过去时
  过去时 → 过去完成时
  will → would    can → could
  例：He said, "I am happy." → He said he was happy.
◆ 时间词变化：
  today → that day
  tomorrow → the next day
  yesterday → the day before
  now → then
◆ 不变的情况：
  客观真理（Water boils...）→ 时态不变
  情态动词（should/could/would/might）→ 不变
